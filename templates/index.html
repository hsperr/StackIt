How to play

  1. Click any empty cell, or one of your own, to drop blocks on it.
  2. An empty cell starts at 3. One of your own cells goes up by 1.
  3. When a cell reaches 5 it topples: it loses 4 blocks and pushes one block into each neighbour.
  4. Every cell it topples into becomes yours. Toppling can set off a chain reaction.
  5. Own every cell on the board and you win.
